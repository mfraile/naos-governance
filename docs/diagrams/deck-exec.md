# NAOS-Governance

Local-first governance-as-code for AI-assisted software delivery.

NAOS-Governance helps teams connect AI-assisted work to specs, tasks, evidence,
gates, dashboards, and human review decisions.

---

# What It Adds

- Professional greenfield and brownfield adoption workflow
- Deterministic local reports and schemas
- Spec-cascade, module-header, and test-evidence traceability surfaces
- Profile-aware gate and readiness posture
- Evidence pack and dashboard summaries
- Explicit claim and human-review boundaries

It is not a runtime AI monitor, certification system, or automatic approval engine.

---

# Executive Decision Point

Use NAOS-Governance when the organization needs reviewable SDLC evidence for
AI-assisted work.

Keep separate controls for runtime monitoring, legal/regulatory decisions,
production incident response, and final approval authority.
